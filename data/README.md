# Input files

`Gene_expression.tsv` is tab-separated, with one row per gene:

- `Gene_ID`: unique gene identifier
- `Gene_Symbol`: display name
- `Position`: genomic interval, such as chr1:100-200
- `<sample>:TPM`: expression columns
- optional `<sample>:Readcount` columns in the same sample order

`clinical_info.csv` requires `B-No` (the matching sample identifier) and `Group`. Optional columns are `Sex`, `Age`, `PMI`, `RIN`, `APOE`, and `Iso-seq`.

Use at least four samples, two groups with at least two samples each, and three variable genes after filtering. Expression must be finite and nonnegative. Sample and gene IDs must be unique and nonempty.

Research inputs belong in the ignored `data/raw/` directory. The files in `data/demo/` are generated by `scripts/generate_demo_data.py` and contain no research records.
